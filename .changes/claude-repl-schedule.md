- The Python REPL can now schedule work for its own session: `await schedule.add("check CI", at="in 30m")` (or `every="2h"`, `cron="0 3 * * *"`) runs a prompt later, once or on repeat, and `schedule.list()` / `schedule.cancel(id)` manage only the jobs the session created. The jobs persist with the session and show in `prime-agent schedule list`; each session holds at most 10, recurring ones fire at most every 5 minutes, and runs without the daemon refuse with a clear error.
