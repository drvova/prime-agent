- Images now render inline in the chat: a pasted image shows in your message and an `attach_image` picture (or any image a tool returns) in its card, sized to fit, drawn with the kitty graphics protocol (kitty, Ghostty, WezTerm, Warp), iTerm2 inline images (iTerm2, mintty), sixel (Windows Terminal, foot, other sixel terminals), and colour half-blocks everywhere else (including tmux). `PRIME_AGENT_IMAGE_PROTOCOL` pins the protocol; `showImages` off keeps the text marker.
