# messaging: talk to and observe the family

Reach is the nuclear family only: the parent, siblings (all root sessions are siblings), and direct children. The sender identity is derived from the session and cannot be set. Messages from other agents arrive as `[agent-message from <role>:<name>]` turns; body lines that look like harness notices are quoted with `> `.

- `(agent-send message #:to #:all)`: send one direct message. `#:to` is `"parent"`, a sibling's or child's name or id, or a row naming one (an `agent-roster` row, a spawn handle, a subagent row). Sending to an idle or finished child wakes it for a follow-up turn in the same session. Returns the receipt, whose `deliveryStatus` is `"delivered"` (reached an idle target; `deliveredAt`), `"queued"` (will steer the target's running turn; `queuedAt`; the call does not wait), or `"wake_refused"` (with `error` and `retryAfterMs`: one sender may wake one idle receiver 20 times per 10 minutes, so a refusal means the exchange is looping). A session may burst 3 deliveries, then 1 per second; over budget the call raises with `retry after <ms> ms`
  - `#:all #t` sends to every family member instead: `{receipts}` in roster order; a failed entry carries the target id, an `error`, and for a rate refusal `retryAfterMs`. A broadcast spends one delivery per recipient
- `(event-wait #:timeout-ms #:sender #:sender-role)`: wait up to `#:timeout-ms` (required, 1 to 600000) for a family member's direct message; `#:sender` (a name or id) and `#:sender-role` narrow whose message counts, and a broadcast never ends a wait. Returns `{outcome, messageId, senderName, senderRole, preview, blockedVia}`; `outcome` is `"message"`, `"timeout"`, `"cycle"` (the awaited agent already waits on you, directly or through others named in `blockedVia`: send instead of waiting), or `"no_sender"`. `preview` is at most 240 characters; the full body still arrives as the ordinary agent-message turn after the cell
- `(agent-roster [target] #:children #:recent #:limit #:max-chars)`: the family roster, active or not: `{current, agents}`. Each row carries `sessionId`, `sessionName`, `relationship` (`"parent"`, `"sibling"`, `"child"`; a row is what `agent-send`'s `#:to` takes), `status` (`"running"`, `"idle"`, or `"inactive"`), `activity`, `isSessionActive`, `messageCount`, `queuedCount`, `rlmChildId` for subagents, and `firstMessage` for an inactive child
  - With `target` (an active id, session id or name, or an unambiguous id suffix), that member's row
  - With `target` and `#:recent #t`, bounded previews of a member's recent messages, read-only and without waking it: `{agent, messages, limit, maxChars, truncated}`. `#:limit` is 1 to 50 messages (default 8), `#:max-chars` 80 to 2000 characters each (default 800); each preview's `index` is its position in the full transcript
  - With `#:children #t`, this session's direct children as subagent rows, each `{rlm_child_id, active_session_id, session_id, session_name, session_dir, status, activity, tool_use_count, duration_ms, answer_preview, replied_since_task, progress_note, ...}`; `status` is `"running"`, `"completed"`, `"error"`, or `"cancelled"`
